1 Mary was calm at first, but _____ into tears all of a sudden. ② turned ① burst `burst into tears` 是固定用法，不用 `turned into tears`。
4 He pressed his fingers against his _____ to ease the headache that was pounding on both sides of his head. ② calves ① temples 頭兩側太陽穴是 `temples`；`calves` 是小腿肚。
5 Before the entire team entered the forest, two experienced hikers were sent ahead to quietly _____ the area and report back on possible dangers and safe paths. ④ haunt ② scout `scout the area` 表示先行探查地區；`haunt` 是出沒、纏繞。
6 A magnitude 5.0 earthquake _____ Tainan in southern Taiwan last night. ① broke ④ struck 地震「襲擊某地」用 `struck`；`broke` 不搭配這種句型。
9 Secondhand smoke _____ contributes to over 40,000 deaths among nonsmoking adults and 400 deaths in infants each year. ③ implementation ② exposure `exposure to secondhand smoke` 才是「暴露於二手菸」。
10 According to data from online movie rental services, people are more likely to pick romance films over other _____ when the temperature drops. ③ characters ④ genres 電影分類用 `genres`；`characters` 是角色，不是片種。
14 Sustainable job creation happens when infrastructure, policies, investment and the public and private _____ all work together. ③ drills ① sectors `public and private sectors` 是固定搭配，指公私部門。
18 The professor _____ lectures impressed the students received an award. ① who ③ whose 後面接名詞 `lectures`，要用所有格關代 `whose`。
19 _____ from the long journey through the mountains, the hikers decided to rest beside the river. ③ Exhausting ② Exhausted 主詞是 hikers，表示「感到疲憊」要用 `Exhausted`。
22 The health impact of any substance can vary _____ depending on its form and dose. ② signification ④ significantly `vary` 是動詞，後面要副詞修飾，所以用 `significantly`。
23 An estimated 2.3 million women _____ diagnosed with breast cancer globally in 2023. ④ will be ② were `in 2023` 是過去時間，且句型是被動式，所以用 `were diagnosed`。
24 Wuling Farm, _____ is home to more than 20,000 cherry blossom trees, imposes seasonal traffic controls during peak bloom periods. ① that ③ which 逗號後是非限定關代子句，不能用 `that`，要用 `which`。
27 Frédéric Chopin, _____, was one of the most celebrated virtuoso pianists of his day. ① who composing in Poland ③ a Polish composer 逗號中間要能成為同位語；`a Polish composer` 結構完整，其他選項文法不通。
31 Working a four-day week has been common across the Netherlands for several years. On _____, Dutch employees work about 32 hours per week... ② duty ① average `on average` 是固定片語，表示「平均而言」。
35 One way to expand their labor _____ could be to increase the number of Dutch women in full-time work. ③ profits ② supply `labor supply` 指勞動供給；`profits` 與上下文不合。
38 However, measures to _____ overtourism and its negative impacts, including friction with residents, are still incomplete. ① comprise ④ address `address a problem` 是「處理問題」；`comprise` 不是這個意思。
39 ... dissatisfaction is mounting _____ the daily congestion of public transportation, including buses. ④ unlike ② over `mounting over` 在這裡是對某事不滿增加；`unlike` 表示不像，語意不通。
