銷售員如何銷售的英文句子

這裡的句子偏向專業、穩健、可合規使用的說法，避免過度承諾或誤導式推銷。

English

Hello, I work with personal loan solutions and I would like to see whether any option may fit your needs.

中文解釋

你好，我負責個人信貸方案，想先了解是否有適合你需求的選項。

English

May I ask if you are looking for cash flexibility, debt consolidation, or a lower monthly payment?

中文解釋

我可以請問你目前是想提高資金彈性、整合債務，還是降低每月還款壓力嗎？

English

Because you work as a pilot, your income profile may be suitable for certain loan programs, subject to review.

中文解釋

因為你的職業是機師，你的收入條件可能適合某些貸款方案，但仍要以審核結果為準。

English

I can help you estimate the loan amount, the repayment term, and the possible monthly payment.

中文解釋

我可以協助你預估可貸額度、還款期限，以及可能的每月還款金額。

English

If you want, I can first explain the basic terms in simple English before we discuss any application details.

中文解釋

如果你願意，我可以先用簡單英文說明基本條件，再進一步談申請細節。

English

The final rate and approval result will depend on document review and the bank's assessment.

中文解釋

最終利率與核准結果，會依文件審查與銀行評估而定。

English

To save your time, I can tell you in advance which documents are usually required for foreign applicants.

中文解釋

為了節省你的時間，我可以先告訴你外籍申請人通常需要準備哪些文件。

English

Our goal is to find a repayment plan that is practical for your current income and schedule.

中文解釋

我們的目標是找出一個符合你目前收入與工作安排的實際還款方案。

English

If you are comparing options, I can help you understand the difference between loan amount, rate, fee, and term.

中文解釋

如果你正在比較不同方案，我可以幫你理解額度、利率、費用與期數之間的差異。

English

Please feel free to ask questions, and I will explain the loan structure as clearly as possible.

中文解釋

有任何問題都可以直接問，我會盡量清楚說明這筆信貸的結構與條件。

English

If the program is not suitable for you, I will let you know honestly instead of pushing an application.

中文解釋

如果這個方案不適合你，我會誠實告知，而不是硬推你申請。

English

Would you like me to give you a quick overview first, or do you prefer to go directly into the numbers?

中文解釋

你希望我先快速介紹整體方案，還是直接跟你談數字條件？
